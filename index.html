FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a repair or reupholstery take?

It depends on the job. Simple mechanism repairs are often done within a few days, while full reupholstery of a set can take longer. We'll give you a clear timeline once we've assessed your piece.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Let us know your location when you reach out and we'll arrange collection and delivery for your furniture.

How much will it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the piece, the extent of the damage, and the fabric or leather you choose. Send us a photo on WhatsApp for a free, no-obligation quote.

What types of furniture do you repair?

Recliners, sofas, loveseats, sectionals and armchairs — in fabric or leather, as single pieces or full living room sets.

Do I need to bring my furniture to your workshop?

Not necessarily — depending on the job, we can assess and repair on-site or arrange pickup and delivery.

Is there a warranty on the work?

We stand behind our workmanship. Ask us about warranty details when you get your quote.
